# Original author and acknowledgements

The PDF supplied at `references/dicionario.pdf` is the primary source for the corpus attribution.

- Page 1: _Dicionário aberto de calão e expressões idiomáticas_, “(recolha de) José João Almeida”, dated 7 October 2026.
- Page 2: introduction describes a collection built with multiple informants, connecting linguistics, programming and Perl, using DPL from Projeto Natura. It includes an extensive list of contributors. The short quotation on the project page is transcribed from this page.
- Pages 3–301: dictionary records imported into the application.

The PDF's reference to approximately 4,000 entries is its own introductory estimate. The website's 7,241 figure counts parsed records, including separate synonym headings, duplicate headwords and incomplete entries; it is not presented as the author's editorial count.

Additional primary sources, checked on 20 October 2026:

- [Centro ALGORITMI, 23 May 2024](https://algoritmi.uminho.pt/news/dicionario-aberto-de-calao-e-expressoes-idiomaticas-de-jose-joao-almeida/): Almeida's teaching and research affiliation; a collection assembled over 30 years and made available online without charge. The biography attributes that duration to the dated report rather than inferring a starting year.
- [Projeto Natura](https://natura.di.uminho.pt/wiki/doku.php?id=ProjectoNatura): Almeida as founding member and the project's Portuguese natural-language processing work.
- [Natura dictionary resources](https://natura.di.uminho.pt/wiki/doku.php?id=dicionarios%3Amain): Jspell developed by Ulisses Pinto and José João Almeida in 1994. Both receive credit in the biography.
- [Almeida and Pedro Rangel Henriques, September 1999](https://natura.di.uminho.pt/~jj/dac.html): the open dictionary's collaborative approach, concept-oriented representation, and usefulness despite incompleteness. The historical document mentions a GNU licence without a version in its example metadata; this does not establish the exact licence terms for the supplied 2026 PDF, and no CC BY-SA label is inferred.
- [Original dictionary](https://natura.di.uminho.pt/~jj/pln/calao/dicionario.html) and [author's academic page](https://natura.di.uminho.pt/~jj/): linked directly from the website.

The tribute expresses this project's appreciation; it does not imply Almeida's endorsement, participation in this interface, or affiliation with Léxico Vivo. The JJ monogram is typographic, not a purported portrait. The supplied generated portrait and the prototype's unsupported Alberto P. de Vasconcelos attribution are not used as factual material.
